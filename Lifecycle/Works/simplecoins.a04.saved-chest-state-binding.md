## Acceptance

- Preserve the approved A04 requirement that SAVED BlockLocker can attribute only a real, physically existing chest/trapped chest from the same selected SourceFile/Chunk. The source-scoped lookup currently validates BlockEntity coordinates and type; also verify **the chosen saved terrain block-state palette at exactly that same position** declares a chest/trapped chest rather than air/other block.
- Do not infer physical chest identity from a left-behind or anomalous BlockEntity alone. Malformed/missing saved sections, wrong chunk identity, non-chest block state, or invalid chest facing/type are HOLD/IOException, not a source-backed Owner or Free.
- Keep existing legacy lookup unchanged. Only opt-in source-scoped SAVED lookup adds same-chunk palette confirmation. No new accepted ownership or world-stability proof; the external BlockLocker owner index still requires independently verified complete physical attachment and source-generation witnesses.
- TDD: first Red on physical air with matching chest BlockEntity; Green by reusing existing SavedTerrainBlockPalette reader, and preserve synthetic selected Anvil success with a fixture containing actual block-state data. No new Production feature unrelated to physical source identity, and no G0 writer-exclusion/publication, G3, Admission, main, VPS.
