## Acceptance

- Reuse Confirmed A04 local SAVED owner verdict and physical support evidence. From only positive OWNER_UNPUBLISHED/FREE_UNPUBLISHED candidate evidence (with separately supplied valid external proof), build a read-only index of (world name, each actual physical chest-half block coordinate) to one local principal/Everyone/non-protection reason. Reject HOLD observations and never substitute no-index-entry as Free.
- A verified reciprocal double-chest group must yield the same single unpublished holder for BOTH physical halves. Detect and reject repeated physical block positions, including a second traversal of the same double chest from the opposite half, without double-counting. Independent worlds with identical coordinates remain distinct.
- Fail the whole pending index after any conflicting or incomplete append: no partially valid lookup may be exposed; freeze the index only after all supplied observations were processed. Return an immutable local index, not a settlement staging transaction or a source/writer/generation proof.
- Keep raw physical keys separate from BlockLocker profile text. Only already verified UUID may be exported for owner candidate; FREE distinguishes effective Everyone from positively proved unprotected. No BAN/playerdata decisions or admission/publish; these require separate authoritative evidence and source generation coherence.
- TDD Red first, minimal implementation, direct/CI JUnit and integration. Do not claim all A04 implementation-conformance or global G0 writer/publish success merely from index acceptance. No modification of Confirmed Process/PI/TCE semantics.
